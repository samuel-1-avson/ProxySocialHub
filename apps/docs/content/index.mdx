## How it works in 30 seconds

<Flow caption="The website only ever sees the provider's home connection, never yours.">
  <FlowStep icon="laptop" title="You (buyer)">Ask to visit a website</FlowStep>
  <FlowStep icon="server" title="ProxyHubb" arrow="request">Checks your pass and picks a home to route through</FlowStep>
  <FlowStep icon="house" title="A provider's home" arrow="forwarded">Their computer fetches the page for you</FlowStep>
  <FlowStep icon="globe" title="The website" arrow="visits">Sees the home's location and sends the page back</FlowStep>
</Flow>
